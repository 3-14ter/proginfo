# Feladatok
## 1. feladat

- 1 hold buza &rarr; 25 mazsa buza, 10h/het, 5$/mazsa
- 1 hold kukorics &rarr; 10 mazsa kikorica, 4h/het, 3$/mazsa
- 1 hold repce &rarr; 8 mazsa repce, 3 ora/het, 2$/mazsa

---

- xb: 1 hold buza
- xk: 1 hold kukorica
- xr 1 hold repce

---
- max $5 * 25x_b + 3 * 10x_k + 2 * 8x_k$
- $x;_p+x_k+x_r <= 10$
- $10x_b+4c_k+3x+r <= 60$
- $2*25x_b <= 10x_k$

- 20 fa ossz

- $2x_a <= x_s$

- $4x_a + 3x_a <= 20$
